The people whose photos taught our tree identifier

The tree identifier on this site was trained on 14,697 openly licensed photographs contributed by naturalists to iNaturalist and published in the iNaturalist Open Data set. Only photos released under CC0 or CC BY licences were used (CC0: 5,480 · CC BY: 9,217). Thank you to every photographer below — the Boundary’s trees are easier to recognise because of you.

The identification model itself runs in your browser and is built on the open-source TensorFlow.js runtime and a MobileNetV3 network architecture (Apache 2.0). No photographs are stored or served from this site; the credits are the whole of what we keep.
