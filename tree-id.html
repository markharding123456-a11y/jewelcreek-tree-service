What Tree Is This?

Free photo identification for Boundary-region trees — right on your phone

Not sure what's growing in your yard? Take a photo or three and our identifier will tell you the species, how sure it is, and what that tree tends to do around here. It knows the trees of the Boundary — ponderosa, Douglas-fir, larch, aspen, cottonwood, birch and the common yard trees.

Identification happens entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded or stored.

For the best result, give it three angles:
  • the bark, close up, at chest height
  • the needles or leaves, close and in focus (cones or fruit if you can see them)
  • the whole tree, from a few steps back

This tool gives an automated, best-effort identification and general information only. It is not an assessment of a tree's health or safety. For anything that concerns you, ask us for a free look.
